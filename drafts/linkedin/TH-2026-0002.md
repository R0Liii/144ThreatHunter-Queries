Drupal's installer is being used to take over live sites (CVE-2026-132165), and CISA added it to KEV on 24 November.
If install.php is reachable, an anonymous attacker can try to reinstall the site under their control. Fixed in 10.6.22, 11.3.22 and 11.4.13.
This Defender XDR hunt looks on Linux web servers for settings.php rewritten by PHP, PHP files dropped under sites/*/files, and shells spawned by PHP workers on the same hosts.
Run it if you host Drupal, even after patching: exploitation was reported from 19 November.
